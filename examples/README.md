# Synthetic Examples

These materials are fictional and must not be treated as empirical interviews.

## Try An Interview

Use the [English interview guide](../guides/interviewleitfaden-organizational-resilience-9-items.en.md)
and the [English participant role card](read-aloud-script-specific-follow-ups.en.md).
Select English before starting the interview. The role card supplies short initial
answers and extra details for relevant follow-up questions. Do not upload the role
card as an interview guide. Only answers actually spoken or submitted belong in
the transcript.

The German and English `organizational-resilience` files are deterministic parser
and integration-test fixtures. Their original wording is intentionally retained.

## Partial Interview Examples

- [English interview stopped after two answers](synthetic-english-two-answers-aborted.md)
