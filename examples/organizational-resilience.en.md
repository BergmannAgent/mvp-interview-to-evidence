# Interview guide: Organizational Resilience

## Introduction

### Main question
What is your role in the organization?

#### Follow-up questions

- How long have you worked in this role?
- Which areas are you responsible for?
- Which other departments do you work with?

## Responding to unexpected events

### Main question
Please describe an unexpected situation that posed a particular challenge to your organization.

#### Follow-up questions

- What exactly happened?
- When did the organization recognize that the situation was critical?
- Who was involved in the response?
- Which decisions were made?
- What worked well?
- Where did difficulties arise?

### Main question
How does your organization prepare for unexpected events?

#### Follow-up questions

- Are formal emergency plans in place?
- Are those plans tested regularly?
- Who is responsible for keeping them up to date?
- Could you give a concrete example?

## Closing

### Main question
Is there anything else relevant to the topic that we have not discussed yet?
