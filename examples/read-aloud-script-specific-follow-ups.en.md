# Read-Aloud Script: Elbwerk Komponenten

This is an invented role card for a voice test, not an upload guide or a completed
research interview. Select the English guide
`guides/interviewleitfaden-organizational-resilience-9-items.en.md`. Read only the **First
answer** to the main question actually asked. Then answer only the specific aspect
the AI asks about. The AI may paraphrase, change the order, or skip questions that
have already been answered.

Extra short answers are provided for freely worded follow-ups. Do not read them
all at once. For facts not described here, say: "I don't remember that precisely."
The script prescribes no score. Only the answers actually spoken during the
interview can subsequently serve as evidence.

## Your Role — Do Not Read Aloud

You are **Marion Bauer, aged 44**, Head of Operations at the fictional Elbwerk
Komponenten GmbH. Five team leads report directly to you; your area has 47
employees in total. There are three assembly lines. A fire at the supplier of
control modules caused a twelve-day supply interruption. One line stopped for
four working days in total. The backlog was cleared after approximately five
weeks. All answers refer to this case.

## General Background

### What is your role within the organization?

**First answer:** I'm Marion Bauer, I'm 44, and I'm Head of Operations at Elbwerk Komponenten.

**If asked about your area:** I look after production, logistics, and quality.

### What responsibilities do you have within the organization?

**First answer:** I'm responsible for day-to-day operations and our ability to deliver.

**If asked about decisions:** During major disruptions, I coordinate the different areas. I make decisions together with their respective heads.

### How many employees report to you?

**First answer:** There are 47 in my area altogether. Five team leads report directly to me.

**If the distinction is unclear:** The 47 include the five team leads. I don't have 47 direct reports.

## Robustness

### What does your organization do under difficult conditions to maintain its position and stability?

**First answer:** First, we try to keep operations running.

**If asked about a concrete measure:** We allocate scarce material to the most urgent orders first.

| If the AI asks this                                             | Say this                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Which specific difficult situation are you thinking of?         | There was a fire at our supplier. Afterwards, we couldn't get control modules.                          |
| Which essential activities continued during that situation?     | Two of our three assembly lines kept running. Shipping and customer support also remained available.    |
| Where did restrictions or interruptions occur during that time? | The third line stopped temporarily. We had to postpone some deliveries.                                 |
| How long did the difficult conditions last?                     | The supply interruption lasted twelve days. It took about five weeks to clear the backlog.              |
| How could you tell how stable operations were during that time? | We checked output and the order backlog every day. Two lines kept running, but output was below normal. |

**If asked about the stoppage:** The affected line stopped for four working days in total, not for all twelve days.

### How does your organization develop different solutions when it faces difficult challenges?

**First answer:** The affected areas get together and collect possible options.

**If asked about selection criteria:** We look at what is technically possible and can be implemented quickly enough.

| If the AI asks this                                                         | Say this                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Which different solution options were actually developed in that situation? | Reallocating stock, buying substitute modules, or outsourcing assembly.                             |
| Which of them were actually put into use?                                   | We reallocated stock and bought substitute modules. We didn't outsource assembly.                   |
| How was the use of these solutions coordinated between the areas involved?  | In a short morning briefing with purchasing, production, engineering, quality, and sales.           |
| Which of the solutions used proved workable in practice?                    | Reallocating stock helped immediately. The substitute modules only worked for some of our products. |

**If asked about outsourcing:** The external partner would have needed too much preparation time.

**If asked about substitute modules:** We had to change the configuration. Switching modules wasn't possible for the special variants.

### How does your organization behave when it risks losing something important?

**First answer:** We try to protect what matters most.

**If asked about the approach:** We spoke to the affected customers early and brought forward partial deliveries.

| If the AI asks this                                                     | Say this                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What exactly was your organization at risk of losing in that situation? | Orders from two important customers. One said they would consider other suppliers if the delay went on too long.             |
| What measures were taken to protect the objective or area at risk?      | We brought forward partial deliveries and coordinated with the customers daily. For one order, we paid for express shipping. |
| How long were these measures continued?                                 | The daily contact continued for about three weeks. Then we returned to our weekly exchange.                                  |
| What could be preserved as a result?                                    | Both customers kept their orders with us. One also went ahead with the planned follow-on project later.                      |
| What could not be preserved despite these measures?                     | Four smaller deliveries were late. We couldn't fully absorb the additional purchasing and shipping costs.                    |

### How does your organization deal with setbacks and difficulties when its existing course is at risk?

**First answer:** We have to revise the plan again.

**If asked for an example:** The first substitute delivery had faulty connectors.

| If the AI asks this                                                    | Say this                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What specific setback put your organization's existing course at risk? | Some substitute modules were faulty. We couldn't simply put that delivery into use.                         |
| How did operations actually continue after this setback?               | Two lines kept running. We held back the new delivery and then checked every module individually.           |
| What changes were made to the subsequent approach?                     | We checked every substitute module individually. The supplier was also asked to check them before shipping. |
| What interruptions or reductions in performance occurred?              | The checks cost us about another shift. The affected line stopped for four working days in total.           |

**If the AI tries to add the durations together:** That extra shift is included in the four working days.

## Agility

### How does your organization act when a quick response is required?

**First answer:** We bring together the people who can make decisions.

**If asked about the decision:** We decided which orders would receive the available material first.

| If the AI asks this                                                                 | Say this                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What recent specific situation required a quick response?                           | The fire at our module supplier. We only had enough material for a few production days.                           |
| How much time passed between recognizing the need for action and making a decision? | About two and a half hours. The news arrived around half past seven, and the decision was made around ten.        |
| How much time then passed before the first measure was implemented?                 | The production schedule was changed about two hours later. The substitute order followed the next morning.        |
| Where did delays occur?                                                             | In the technical checks and approval of the higher costs. At first, it wasn't clear who had final responsibility. |

### How does your organization develop and use alternatives to remain able to act or emerge stronger from adverse circumstances?

**First answer:** We tried to become less dependent on one supplier.

**If asked what was implemented:** We approved a second manufacturer for some of our products.

| If the AI asks this                                                                       | Say this                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Which alternative was actually used in response to the adverse situation?                 | A control module from a second manufacturer. Engineering and quality carried out additional checks.    |
| If several alternatives were used, how were they combined, if at all?                     | Substitute modules for standard products, and old stock for the special variants.                      |
| What changed compared with the previous approach as a result of using these alternatives? | We now had two sources for standard variants instead of one. Planning became more complicated, though. |
| Which of these changes remained in place over the longer term?                            | The second supplier is still approved. We now check critical stocks every week.                        |
| What disadvantages or limitations emerged with the alternatives chosen?                   | The substitute modules cost about eight percent more and don't fit the special variants.               |

**If asked about the remaining dependency:** We still don't have an equivalent second source for the special variants.

### How does your organization adapt its actions when the situation changes and concrete measures are needed?

**First answer:** We change priorities and reassign tasks.

**If asked who is involved:** The affected team leads coordinate that with one another.

| If the AI asks this                                                                 | Say this                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What specific change did you have to adjust your approach to?                       | Fewer modules were available than expected. Some customers also changed the order in which they needed deliveries.    |
| Which tasks, responsibilities, or resources were actually adapted as a result?      | Two people moved from assembly to module testing. One person in purchasing focused only on substitute supplies.       |
| How were these adaptations coordinated between the areas affected?                  | Between team leads in the morning, then in the shifts. Major changes were recorded in the shared production schedule. |
| Where could a necessary adaptation not be implemented, or only be implemented late? | The late shift received a changed order sequence hours later. The material records also hadn't been updated at first. |

## Integrity

### How do the employees of your organization commit themselves to doing what is needed in difficult situations?

**First answer:** Many helped wherever extra hands were needed.

**If asked for an example:** People from production helped test the substitute modules.

| If the AI asks this                                                              | Say this                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Which necessary tasks did employees actually take on in the situation described? | Additional module checks and organizing partial deliveries. Experienced machine setters helped with the technical trials.           |

**If asked about the process:** Daily priorities were set with team leads and discussed in the shifts.

## Optional Error Branches — Do Not Use All of Them in One Interview

Each branch replaces the normal first answer only once. Wait for the reaction,
then use the appropriate answer above for the repeated or clarified question.
Do not invent additional events.

### Off-Topic Answer

For the question about different solutions, first say:

> I went hiking at the weekend, and the weather was really nice.

Expected behavior: The AI returns neutrally to the guide question once rather
than exploring the weekend. Then give the normal first answer about solutions.

### Misunderstood Question

For the question about acting together, first say:

> Do you mean whether we do things together after work? I didn't understand that.

Expected behavior: The AI repeats or explains the current question once. Then
give the normal first answer about collaboration.

### Vague Answer for a Freely Worded Follow-Up

For the question about adapting actions, first say:

> We did it differently after that.

Expected behavior: A specific follow-up about action, responsibility, or sequence.
Use the matching detail answer in that section.

### An Aspect That Has Already Been Answered

For the first Robustness question, you may say instead of the first answer:

> After the fire, we couldn't get control modules for twelve days. Two lines kept running, while the third stopped for four working days in total. We cleared the backlog after about five weeks.

Expected behavior: The AI doesn't ask for the same durations or lines again.
A question about material allocation or observed results remains appropriate.
If asked: "We allocated the remaining modules according to order urgency and
checked output and the backlog every day."

### A Short but Complete Answer

If the AI asks again about the number of employees, say only:

> 47 in total, including five direct reports.

Expected behavior: This counts as a concrete answer. Brevity alone isn't a reason
for another follow-up.
