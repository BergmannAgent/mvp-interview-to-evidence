## Methodology

The evaluation codes only evidence from final participant turns against the seven profile anchors. Dimension and overall scores are calculated server-side as arithmetic means of scored items; non-scores are excluded from the means.

### Limitations

- A single interview evidences the interviewee's statements, not organization-wide truth.
- Missing information is not observed negative evidence and must not be treated as a low score.
- Profile anchors and coder output do not constitute an independently psychometrically validated scale and require independent assessment in the application context.
- Partial aggregates must be interpreted together with the number of scored and unscored items.
