# Interview guide: Organizational Resilience - 9 Items

## General Background

### Main question

What is your role within the organization?

### Main question

What responsibilities do you have within the organization?

### Main question

How many employees report to you?

## Robustness

### Main question

What does your organization do under difficult conditions to maintain its position and stability?

#### Follow-up questions

- Which specific difficult situation are you thinking of?
- Which essential activities continued during that situation?
- Where did restrictions or interruptions occur during that time?
- How long did the difficult conditions last?
- How could you tell how stable operations were during that time?

### Main question

How does your organization develop different solutions when it faces difficult challenges?

#### Follow-up questions

- Which different solution options were actually developed in that situation?
- Which of them were actually put into use?
- How was the use of these solutions coordinated between the areas involved?
- Which of the solutions used proved workable in practice?

### Main question

How does your organization behave when it risks losing something important?

#### Follow-up questions

- What exactly was your organization at risk of losing in that situation?
- What measures were taken to protect the objective or area at risk?
- How long were these measures continued?
- What could be preserved as a result?
- What could not be preserved despite these measures?

### Main question

How does your organization deal with setbacks and difficulties when its existing course is at risk?

#### Follow-up questions

- What specific setback put your organization's existing course at risk?
- How did operations actually continue after this setback?
- What changes were made to the subsequent approach?
- What interruptions or reductions in performance occurred?

## Agility

### Main question

How does your organization act when a quick response is required?

#### Follow-up questions

- What recent specific situation required a quick response?
- How much time passed between recognizing the need for action and making a decision?
- How much time then passed before the first measure was implemented?
- Where did delays occur?

### Main question

How does your organization develop and use alternatives to remain able to act or emerge stronger from adverse circumstances?

#### Follow-up questions

- Which alternative was actually used in response to the adverse situation?
- If several alternatives were used, how were they combined, if at all?
- What changed compared with the previous approach as a result of using these alternatives?
- Which of these changes remained in place over the longer term?
- What disadvantages or limitations emerged with the alternatives chosen?

### Main question

How does your organization adapt its actions when the situation changes and concrete measures are needed?

#### Follow-up questions

- What specific change did you have to adjust your approach to?
- Which tasks, responsibilities, or resources were actually adapted as a result?
- How were these adaptations coordinated between the areas affected?
- Where could a necessary adaptation not be implemented, or only be implemented late?

## Integrity

### Main question

How do the employees of your organization commit themselves to doing what is needed in difficult situations?

#### Follow-up questions

- Which necessary tasks did employees actually take on in the situation described?
- How was it established who was responsible for which tasks?
- How did the taking on of tasks differ between groups of employees?
- Which tasks involved difficulties or unclear responsibilities?

### Main question

How does your organization act together with its employees when a coordinated response is required?

#### Follow-up questions

- Which areas had to work together in that situation?
- How were shared priorities agreed between these areas?
- What did collaboration look like in a specific joint decision?
- Where did differences in approach or coordination problems arise between the areas involved?
